WhatsApp CRM questions, answered

What is WhatsApp CRM & Conversational Commerce?

WhatsApp CRM & Conversational Commerce is a multi-user WhatsApp CRM that turns WhatsApp Business into a governed sales, service and commerce channel with routing, automation and analytics. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is WhatsApp CRM for?

It is built for Marketing Head / Growth Lead, Sales / Service Head and Owner / Founder of SME in South Africa.

What problem does WhatsApp CRM solve?

Customer conversations are distributed across personal phones, enquiries have no owner, campaigns lack outcome attribution and routine service consumes staff time. The usual business impact is slow response, lost leads, inconsistent support, compliance and opt-in risk, low campaign conversion and no customer history.

What can WhatsApp CRM help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce first response time, reduce unowned chats, improve lead conversion and reduce manual handling. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does WhatsApp CRM work?

It follows a simple flow: acquire, understand intent, assign owner, complete action and measure. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does WhatsApp CRM integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Meta WhatsApp Business API, Shopify / WooCommerce, Website forms, PayFast, Yoco or Ozow, CRM / EHR / ERP and OpenAI / RAG. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with WhatsApp CRM?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a WhatsApp Lead Conversion pilot for one WhatsApp number and one live campaign.

How much does WhatsApp CRM cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
