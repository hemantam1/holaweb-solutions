Fleet Intelligence questions, answered

What is Fleet Intelligence OS?

Fleet Intelligence OS is a fleet analytics layer over telematics that combines trip, fuel, maintenance and cost data to rank fuel and downtime leakage and assign action. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Fleet Intelligence for?

It is built for Fleet Director / Operations Head, CFO / Procurement Head and Dispatcher / Fleet Supervisor in South Africa.

What problem does Fleet Intelligence solve?

GPS, fuel, maintenance, jobs and finance data are separate; teams react to breakdowns and investigate fuel leakage after costs are incurred. The usual business impact is fuel loss, unplanned downtime, rising cost per km, missed service, poor driver accountability and inefficient routes and assets.

What can Fleet Intelligence help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce fuel variance, reduce downtime, improve maintenance compliance and reduce cost per kilometre. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Fleet Intelligence work?

It follows a simple flow: ingest, compare, rank exceptions, assign action and verify result. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Fleet Intelligence integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including GPS / telematics, Fuel sensors and cards, Maps, Workshop and vendors, Accounting and Mobile driver app. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Fleet Intelligence?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Fleet Leakage diagnostic for a defined group of vehicles.

How much does Fleet Intelligence cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
