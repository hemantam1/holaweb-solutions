Retail POS and Warehouse questions, answered

What is Retail POS & Warehouse OS?

Retail POS & Warehouse OS is an offline-first retail POS and warehouse system that keeps stores trading through load shedding and connection drops, then reconciles prices, stock and sales centrally. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Retail POS and Warehouse for?

It is built for Retail Owner / COO, Store Operations Head and CFO / Retail IT Head in South Africa.

What problem does Retail POS and Warehouse solve?

Stores must keep trading through load shedding and unreliable connectivity, while head office needs central control of prices, stock, approvals and reconciliation. The usual business impact is checkout downtime, stale prices, stock variance, duplicate sync, uncontrolled negative stock and delayed financial visibility.

What can Retail POS and Warehouse help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce checkout downtime, reduce reconciliation time, reduce stock variance and reduce sync exceptions. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Retail POS and Warehouse work?

It follows a simple flow: central update, local operation, queue safely, synchronize and reconcile. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Retail POS and Warehouse integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Card machines and Yoco, Barcode and scales, Sage or Xero, Ecommerce, Biometric and Central ERP / database. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Retail POS and Warehouse?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Offline Store Reconciliation shadow pilot for one store, run alongside your current system.

How much does Retail POS and Warehouse cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
