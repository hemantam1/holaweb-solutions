CRM and Sales Pipeline questions, answered

What is CRM & Sales Pipeline?

CRM & Sales Pipeline is a lean web and mobile CRM for growing sales teams, with configurable stages, next-action discipline and management visibility without a heavy implementation. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is CRM and Sales Pipeline for?

It is built for Founder / Managing Director, Sales Head / Revenue Operations and Sales Representatives / Field Team in South Africa.

What problem does CRM and Sales Pipeline solve?

Leads enter through websites, referrals, calls and WhatsApp, but ownership, next action and forecast discipline are inconsistent. The usual business impact is leads leak, longer sales cycles, founders chase updates, inaccurate forecasts and poor marketing-to-sales handover.

What can CRM and Sales Pipeline help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce lead response time, improve follow-up completion, improve stage conversion and reduce sales cycle. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does CRM and Sales Pipeline work?

It follows a simple flow: capture, qualify, plan next action, coach and progress and close and learn. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does CRM and Sales Pipeline integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Website / WordPress, Email, Gmail / Outlook, WhatsApp, Sage or Xero and Power BI. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with CRM and Sales Pipeline?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Lead Leakage and Pipeline Discipline pilot for one sales team.

How much does CRM and Sales Pipeline cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
