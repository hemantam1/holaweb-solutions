Healthcare Intelligence questions, answered

What is Healthcare Engagement & Intelligence OS?

Healthcare Engagement & Intelligence OS is a patient engagement and follow-up platform that connects EHR and appointment data to WhatsApp care journeys, so clinics and hospitals cut no-shows and complete more follow-ups. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Healthcare Intelligence for?

It is built for Doctor / Practice Owner, Clinic or Day Hospital Manager and Hospital Group IT Head in South Africa.

What problem does Healthcare Intelligence solve?

Patient information, appointments and follow-ups sit in separate systems and personal WhatsApp threads. Teams cannot see overdue care, no-shows or patients likely to disengage. The usual business impact is lost repeat visits, delayed care, front-desk effort, inconsistent patient experience and clinical and revenue risk.

What can Healthcare Intelligence help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce no-show rate, improve follow-up completion, reduce manual reminder time and improve repeat visits. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Healthcare Intelligence work?

It follows a simple flow: patient data, risk and gaps, next action, human approval and measured outcome. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Healthcare Intelligence integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Practice management or HIS, Lab and radiology, WhatsApp, Medical aid claims, Google / Outlook and HL7 / FHIR / API / CSV. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Healthcare Intelligence?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Patient Follow-up and Clinic Growth pilot for one specialty or location.

How much does Healthcare Intelligence cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
