Accounting and Finance questions, answered

What is Accounting & Business Finance?

Accounting & Business Finance is an accounting and business finance system that connects operational transactions to banking, tax and management reporting with fast reconciliation. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Accounting and Finance for?

It is built for Owner / CFO, Financial Controller / Accountant and Multi-company / Operations Head in South Africa.

What problem does Accounting and Finance solve?

Sales, purchases, inventory, banking and tax data are disconnected; management waits for spreadsheets and reconciliations to understand cash and profitability. The usual business impact is slow close, overdue receivables, cash surprises, stock and accounting mismatch, compliance risk and limited multi-company visibility.

What can Accounting and Finance help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce days to close, reduce open reconciliation items, reduce overdue receivables and reduce reporting lag. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Accounting and Finance work?

It follows a simple flow: capture source, approve and post, match, resolve exceptions and report. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Accounting and Finance integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Bank / Netcash, POS, Payroll, Ecommerce, CRM, Inventory, SARS VAT and eFiling exports and Power BI. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Accounting and Finance?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Finance Process and Reconciliation diagnostic for a sanitized sample of your own transactions.

How much does Accounting and Finance cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
