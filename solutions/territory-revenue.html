Territory Revenue OS questions, answered

What is Territory Revenue OS?

Territory Revenue OS is an offline-first field sales and beat planning app that gives reps a daily workflow and managers a live view of visits, orders, stock and missed outlets. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Territory Revenue OS for?

It is built for Sales Director / National Sales Head, Distributor Owner / COO and ERP / IT Head in South Africa.

What problem does Territory Revenue OS solve?

Plans exist, but actual coverage of modern trade, general trade and spaza shops, productive calls, merchandising and secondary orders are not visible reliably, especially when signal is weak or the power is out. The usual business impact is missed outlets, low sales productivity, idle time, lost secondary sales, poor scheme execution and decisions on delayed reports.

What can Territory Revenue OS help us improve?

Teams use it to improve outlet coverage, improve productive calls, improve order value and reduce idle time. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Territory Revenue OS work?

It follows a simple flow: plan, execute offline, synchronize, detect gaps and manager action. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Territory Revenue OS integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Sage, SAP or Odoo, Maps and GPS, WhatsApp retailer ordering, Power BI and Product, stock and price masters. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Territory Revenue OS?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Beat Efficiency and Retailer Growth pilot for one region and a focused team of reps.

How much does Territory Revenue OS cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
