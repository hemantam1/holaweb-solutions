Construction Operations questions, answered

What is Construction Operations OS?

Construction Operations OS is a construction operations system that connects site requisitions, approvals, materials, fuel and labour to project cost, so margin risk shows before month end. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Construction Operations for?

It is built for CEO / CFO, Project Manager and Procurement / Site Stores Head in South Africa.

What problem does Construction Operations solve?

Project budgets, committed costs, purchases, materials, fuel and site labour are updated at different times, so margin leakage appears too late. The usual business impact is budget overrun, duplicate or unapproved purchase, material loss, fuel misuse, delayed approvals and weak project cash visibility.

What can Construction Operations help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce approval cycle, reduce cost leakage, reduce material variance and improve cost visibility. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Construction Operations work?

It follows a simple flow: plan, request, approve and buy, receive and use and control margin. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Construction Operations integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Sage or ERP, Biometric, Fleet and fuel, Document repository, Supplier portal and Payments. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.
