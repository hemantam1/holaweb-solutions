Payroll and HRMS questions, answered

What is Payroll, HRMS & Document Control?

Payroll, HRMS & Document Control is a payroll, HRMS and employee document system that catches attendance and payroll exceptions before release and tracks document expiry. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Payroll and HRMS for?

It is built for HR Director, Payroll / Finance Head and Compliance / Internal Audit in South Africa.

What problem does Payroll and HRMS solve?

Attendance, policies, leave, payroll inputs and employee documents are disconnected, causing late exceptions and weak audit evidence. The usual business impact is payroll errors, employee disputes, compliance exposure, manual checking, late settlements and missed document expiry.

What can Payroll and HRMS help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce preparation time, improve early exception detection, reduce post-payroll corrections and reduce expiry incidents. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Payroll and HRMS work?

It follows a simple flow: collect inputs, detect exceptions, approve, calculate and reconcile and release and retain. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Payroll and HRMS integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Biometric devices, Banking / Netcash, Sage or Xero, Email, Identity, Document repository and SARS eFiling exports. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Payroll and HRMS?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Payroll Readiness shadow run for one entity, run alongside your current payroll.

How much does Payroll and HRMS cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
