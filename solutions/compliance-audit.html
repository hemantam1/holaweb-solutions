Audit and Traceability OS questions, answered

What is Compliance, Audit & Traceability OS?

Compliance, Audit & Traceability OS is an audit, CAPA and traceability platform that takes inspections from mobile evidence capture through risk scoring to verified closure and QR source proof. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Audit and Traceability OS for?

It is built for Chief Compliance / Quality Officer, Audit Firm Owner / Operations Head and Buyer / Regulator / Supply-chain Head in South Africa.

What problem does Audit and Traceability OS solve?

Audit evidence is scattered, corrective actions remain open and management cannot distinguish routine backlog from material risk or prove source claims end to end. The usual business impact is repeat findings, delayed certification, operational incidents, weak regulator and buyer confidence and high report-preparation effort.

What can Audit and Traceability OS help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce closure cycle, reduce overdue findings, reduce report preparation and improve evidence completeness. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Audit and Traceability OS work?

It follows a simple flow: inspect, assess risk, assign action, verify closure and prove trust. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Audit and Traceability OS integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Document repositories, ERP, Identity / SSO, IoT and QR, Government portals and BI. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Audit and Traceability OS?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Audit Backlog and Closure pilot for one audit program or site.

How much does Audit and Traceability OS cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
