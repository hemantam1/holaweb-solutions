Ecommerce Intelligence questions, answered

What is Ecommerce & Marketplace Intelligence?

Ecommerce & Marketplace Intelligence is a cross-channel ecommerce analytics platform that unifies marketplace and webstore data into alerts and owned actions for stock, price, promotions and margin. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Ecommerce Intelligence for?

It is built for Brand / Ecommerce Director, Marketplace / Category Manager and CFO / Commercial Head in South Africa.

What problem does Ecommerce Intelligence solve?

Sales, price, inventory, promotion and catalogue data are split across Takealot, Amazon.co.za, Makro Marketplace, Bash, Shopify, WooCommerce and spreadsheets. The usual business impact is stock-outs and oversell, slow price response, unclear promotion roi, catalogue errors, fragmented profitability and manual reporting.

What can Ecommerce Intelligence help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce stock-out days, improve promotion return, reduce reporting time and reduce margin leakage. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Ecommerce Intelligence work?

It follows a simple flow: collect channels, reconcile, detect exceptions, assign action and measure outcome. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Ecommerce Intelligence integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Marketplace APIs and feeds, Shopify, WooCommerce, ERP / PIM / WMS, Advertising and BI. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Ecommerce Intelligence?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Cross-channel Revenue Leakage diagnostic for one brand or category.

How much does Ecommerce Intelligence cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
