Agriculture Traceability questions, answered

What is Agriculture Production & Traceability Platform?

Agriculture Production & Traceability Platform is a farm-to-batch traceability platform that records farms, crops, practices, inspections and procurement, and links every lot to a QR history buyers can trust. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Agriculture Traceability for?

It is built for Farm or Packhouse Manager, Food Brand / Buyer / Procurement Head and Agri Development Program Director in South Africa.

What problem does Agriculture Traceability solve?

Farm, practice, input, inspection, production and procurement records are fragmented; yield and source claims cannot be verified consistently. The usual business impact is procurement uncertainty, rejected lots, compliance risk, weak buyer trust, limited premium access and poor program reporting.

What can Agriculture Traceability help us improve?

Teams use it to improve evidence completeness, reduce forecast error, reduce rejected lots and improve traceable volume. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Agriculture Traceability work?

It follows a simple flow: register source, record practice, inspect, procure and test and trace batch. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Agriculture Traceability integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including GIS / PostGIS, Weather, Sensors, WhatsApp, QR, Weighing and procurement, ERP and Government portals. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Agriculture Traceability?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Farm-to-Batch Traceability pilot for one crop cluster.

How much does Agriculture Traceability cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
