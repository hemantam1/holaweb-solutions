Property Management questions, answered

What is Property & Community Management?

Property & Community Management is a property and community management platform for sectional title schemes, estates and rentals that gives managing agents, trustees, residents and vendors one record for requests, levies and documents. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Property Management for?

It is built for Managing Agent / Portfolio Manager, Estate or Facility Manager and Body Corporate or HOA Trustees in South Africa.

What problem does Property Management solve?

Unit, owner, tenant, levy, visitor, service and document records are fragmented across spreadsheets, calls and resident WhatsApp groups. The usual business impact is slow issue resolution, unpaid levies, weak resident experience, poor handover, limited portfolio visibility and document risk.

What can Property Management help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce resolution time, reduce overdue levies, reduce repeat issues and reduce status calls. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Property Management work?

It follows a simple flow: receive request, classify, assign work, verify evidence and measure. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Property Management integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including PayFast and debit orders, Sage or Xero, Access control, Email / WhatsApp, Document storage and Facility vendors. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Property Management?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Property Service and Collections pilot for one property or community.

How much does Property Management cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
