Hyperlocal Marketplace questions, answered

What is Hyperlocal Digital Marketplace?

Hyperlocal Digital Marketplace is a multi-vendor hyperlocal marketplace platform with customer and vendor apps, WhatsApp ordering, payments, commissions and delivery coordination, launched zone by zone. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Hyperlocal Marketplace for?

It is built for Marketplace Sponsor / City or Community Operator, Anchor Supplier / Distributor and Local Vendor / Merchant Network in South Africa.

What problem does Hyperlocal Marketplace solve?

Local vendors, suppliers and customers are digitally disconnected; discovery, availability, ordering and fulfilment happen through fragmented calls and chats. The usual business impact is low vendor reach, high coordination cost, weak order visibility, failed fulfilment and hard to reach marketplace density.

What can Hyperlocal Marketplace help us improve?

Teams use it to improve vendor activation, improve order conversion, improve fulfilment success and improve repeat orders. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Hyperlocal Marketplace work?

It follows a simple flow: build supply, publish availability, create demand, fulfil and scale zone. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Hyperlocal Marketplace integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including Maps, PayFast, Yoco or Ozow, Logistics, WhatsApp, ERP / POS, Identity and Analytics. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Hyperlocal Marketplace?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Zone Launch Blueprint and MVP pilot for one launch zone, starting with a launch blueprint.

How much does Hyperlocal Marketplace cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
