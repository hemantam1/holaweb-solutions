Skill Intelligence and LMS questions, answered

What is Skill Intelligence & LMS?

Skill Intelligence & LMS is a skill intelligence and learning platform that maps roles to skills, assigns targeted practice including AI role-play, and measures job readiness. Holaweb delivers it in South Africa on the Infooware platform, starting with a fixed-scope pilot shaped around your own problems and data.

Who is Skill Intelligence and LMS for?

It is built for CHRO / L&D Head, School / Training Provider Leader and Sales / Service Operations Head in South Africa.

What problem does Skill Intelligence and LMS solve?

Organizations track course completion but cannot prove role capability, practice quality or readiness for real work. The usual business impact is training spend without performance change, slow onboarding, inconsistent service and sales, certification failure and manager uncertainty.

What can Skill Intelligence and LMS help us improve?

Teams use it to reduce time to readiness, improve assessment score, improve practice completion and reduce coaching effort. We measure where you are today during discovery and agree the targets with you before any pilot starts.

How does Skill Intelligence and LMS work?

It follows a simple flow: define role, diagnose, assign path, simulate and confirm readiness. People keep the decisions that need judgment, and every outcome is measured.

What does Skill Intelligence and LMS integrate with?

It works alongside your existing systems, including HRMS, SSO, SCORM / video, CRM / field sales, Knowledge base / RAG and Conferencing. Nothing has to be replaced to get started.

How do we get started with Skill Intelligence and LMS?

Start with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone at +91 63596 93055. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, then propose a Skill Gap and AI Practice pilot for one critical role.

How much does Skill Intelligence and LMS cost?

Pricing depends on your scope, users, sites and integrations. We share it once we understand your requirements, after a discovery conversation.
