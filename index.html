Questions about Holaweb

What does Holaweb offer?

Holaweb brings Infooware operating software to South African businesses: solutions for field sales and distribution, retail, payroll, finance, fleet, construction, compliance, healthcare, property, agriculture, learning and ecommerce. Each combines software, AI agents that handle repetitive steps, and named experts who own judgment and outcomes.

Who builds the software?

The platform is built by Infooware. Holaweb is the local partner that understands your business, configures the solution, trains your team and supports you day to day.

Where in South Africa does Holaweb work?

Holaweb works with businesses across South Africa, including Gauteng, the Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal and the Eastern Cape.

Does it keep working during load shedding?

Yes. Field sales, retail POS, audit, agriculture and construction solutions capture work offline on mobile devices and local databases, then synchronise safely when power or connectivity returns.

How does working with Holaweb start?

With a conversation. We learn your problems, gaps and requirements, look at how the work happens today, and then design a fixed-scope pilot with a named owner and an agreed measure of success.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on scope, users, sites and integrations, so we share it after understanding your requirements in a discovery conversation.

Does it replace Sage, Xero or our ERP?

No. The solutions run alongside Sage, Xero, SAP, Odoo, POS, payroll and ecommerce systems and connect through APIs, files and WhatsApp.

How is customer data handled?

Workflows are designed with POPIA in mind, with consent capture, role-based access and audit trails. We review your data-handling requirements during discovery.
